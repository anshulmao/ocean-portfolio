iii. · Energy product · Independent build

WattMana

An electricity bill should explain itself.

RoleIndependent product design and working prototypes
BuiltWeb and mobile prototypes
FocusRinggit and kWh together
LinksGitHub profile ↗

The problem

A dense electricity bill makes it difficult to understand what changed and which household decisions might matter.

The decision

Start with the bill story, show ringgit and kWh together, and keep modelled device costs visibly separate from measurements on the bill.

What changed

Working web and mobile prototypes connect bill explanation, household use and learning. Device costs remain estimates with visible confidence ranges.

NoteDevice costs are modelled estimates, not meter observations.
SectionsHome, Bills, My Use and Learn.

“An explanation earns trust when the interface makes the source and uncertainty of each number visible.”
