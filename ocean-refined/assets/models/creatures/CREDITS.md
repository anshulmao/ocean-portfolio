# Separated creature assets

Sea creatures: Lhimo, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sea-creatures-a9e579418f36468ba33902eeb8d9d96a

Magic ocean: mister_monopoly, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/magic-ocean-f595e1812f0f42e0a4ce16acc0ac7107

Both source packs identify CC BY-SA 4.0 in their embedded metadata: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The extracted GLB adaptations remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: components separated, geometry centered and normalized, material roughness/metalness adjusted, six-joint skin with weighted vertices added, gentle looped swim clips added, textures limited to 1024px. `tools/prepare-creatures.js` preserves the regeneration recipe. No existing source animations or rigs were present.

The realistic fin whale, manta, hammerhead and orca are used as slow visitors. Two smaller magic rays are used on outer/deeper routes. Other extracted magic assets are available for inspection, including the decorative reef carrier, which is excluded from runtime visitors.
