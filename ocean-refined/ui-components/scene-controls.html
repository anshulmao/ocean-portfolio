Lighting & movement

Tune while you explore. Click the ocean to aim above or below the surface; Esc frees your cursor. W and S swim, A and D turn, Space rises and Shift descends. Hold E while swimming for a scooter boost; Q toggles the flashlight. V switches between first person and a camera following the diver. On a touch screen the thumb-stick swims and a drag looks; a gamepad's sticks do the same. Pick a control feel below, then fine-tune it.
